**3. The Table**

*   **Column Headers:** VIRAL FAMILY | ENVELOPE | DNA STRUCTURE | MEDICAL IMPORTANCE

*   **Row 1: Herpesviruses**
    *   **Envelope:** Yes
    *   **DNA Structure:** DS and linear
    *   **Medical Importance:** See Herpesviruses entry

*   **Row 2: Poxvirus**
    *   **Envelope:** Yes
    *   **DNA Structure:** DS and linear (largest DNA virus)
    *   **Medical Importance:** Smallpox eradicated world wide by use of the live-attenuated vaccine; Cowpox (“milkmaid blisters”); **Molluscum contagiosum**—flesh-colored papule with central umbilication; keratinocytes contain molluscum bodies **A**

*   **Row 3: Hepadnavirus**
    *   **Envelope:** Yes
    *   **DNA Structure:** Partially DS and circular
    *   **Medical Importance:** HBV:
        *   Acute or chronic hepatitis
        *   Not a retrovirus but has reverse transcriptase

*   **Row 4: Adenovirus**
    *   **Envelope:** No
    *   **DNA Structure:** DS and linear
    *   **Medical Importance:** Febrile pharyngitis **B**—sore throat; Acute hemorrhagic cystitis; Pneumonia; Conjunctivitis—“pink eye”; Gastroenteritis; Myocarditis

*   **Row 5: Papillomavirus**
    *   **Envelope:** No
    *   **DNA Structure:** DS and circular
    *   **Medical Importance:** HPV—warts, cancer (cervical, anal, penile, or oropharyngeal); serotypes 1, 2, 6, 11 associated with warts; serotypes 16, 18 associated with cancer

*   **Row 6: Polyomavirus**
    *   **Envelope:** No
    *   **DNA Structure:** DS and circular
    *   **Medical Importance:** JC virus—progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy (PML) in immunocompromised patients (eg, HIV); BK virus—transplant patients, commonly targets kidney; **JC: Junky Cerebrum; BK: Bad Kidney**

*   **Row 7: Parvovirus**
    *   **Envelope:** No
    *   **DNA Structure:** SS and linear (smallest DNA virus; *parvus* = small)
    *   **Medical Importance:** B19 virus—aplastic crises in sickle cell disease, “slapped cheek” rash in children (erythema infectiosum, or fifth disease); infects RBC precursors and endothelial cells → RBC destruction → hydrops fetalis and death in fetus, pure RBC aplasia and rheumatoid arthritis–like symptoms in adults
