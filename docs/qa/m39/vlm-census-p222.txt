**1) Replication-Cycle Figure Description**
The figure is a **line art schematic diagram** of a hepatocyte cell. Its visual components include:
*   **Cell Boundary:** A large, rounded rectangular outline representing the Hepatocyte, with the interior labeled "Cytoplasm."
*   **Organelles:** A blue, irregularly shaped **Nucleus** in the center; a surrounding blue network labeled **ER**; a green, stacked structure on the right labeled **Golgi apparatus**; and a cluster of small blue circles at the bottom right labeled **Membranous web [derived from ER]**.
*   **Virion:** The HCV particle is depicted as a purple circle containing squiggly lines (RNA), surrounded by a spiky outer layer labeled **Lipoprotein**.
*   **Cycle Elements:** Numbered steps (1–6) connected by black arrows showing the flow: **Receptor binding and endocytosis**, **Fusion/uncoating/RNA release**, **Translation** (at a **Ribosome**), **Replication**, **Assembly**, **Maturation**, and **Release**. RNA strands are shown as `(+ ) RNA` and `~(- ) RNA`.

**2) Drug Label Placement in Figure**
*   **NS5A inhibitors (Elbasvir, Ledipasvir, Pibrentasvir, Velpatasvir):** Positioned in a box above **Step 4 (Assembly)**, with an inhibition symbol (circle-slash) pointing to the assembly process.
*   **NS3/4A inhibitors (Glecaprevir, Grazoprevir):** Positioned in a box below **Step 2 (Translation)**, with an inhibition symbol pointing to the translation/ribosome area.
*   **NS5B inhibitors (Sofosbuvir):** Positioned in a box next to **Step 3 (Replication)**, with an inhibition symbol pointing to the membranous web/replication area.
*   **Ribavirin:** Labeled inside the **Nucleus**, with an inhibition symbol pointing directly to the **IMP → GMP** conversion step.

**3) IMP → GMP → GTP Strip Location and Content**
*   **Location:** Inside the **Nucleus** of the hepatocyte schematic.
*   **Content:** It shows a horizontal biochemical pathway: **IMP** is converted to **GMP**, which is then converted to **GTP**. 
*   **Mechanism:** It illustrates that Ribavirin acts as a guanine nucleotide analog by competitively inhibiting **IMP dehydrogenase** (the enzyme converting IMP to GMP), thereby depleting GTP pools required for viral RNA replication.
