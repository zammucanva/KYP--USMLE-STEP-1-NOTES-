## Key facts per topic (from text layer)
- **gene-expression-modifications**: transgenic mice = random insertion (constitutive expression) vs targeted insertion/deletion via homologous recombination (conditional expression); knock-out = removing a gene; knock-in = inserting a gene. RNA interference = small non-coding RNAs target mRNAs to inhibit gene expression. miRNA: naturally produced as hairpin; LOOSE pairing → broad targeting of related mRNAs; blocks translation, sometimes facilitates degradation; abnormal miRNA expression contributes to malignancies (silencing tumor-suppressor mRNA). siRNA: usually exogenous dsRNA (eg, virus); requires COMPLETE pairing → highly specific targeting; results in mRNA cleavage prior to translation; used for gene "knockdown" (transcribed or chemically synthesized).
- **genetic terms p76**: codominance (blood groups A/B/AB; α1-antitrypsin deficiency; HLA), variable expressivity (NF1 varying severity), incomplete penetrance (BRCA1; % penetrance × probability of inheriting genotype = risk of expressing phenotype), pleiotropy (CF: lung/GI thick mucus, pancreatic insufficiency, male infertility), anticipation (trinucleotide repeats, Huntington), loss of heterozygosity (tumor suppressor — wild-type allele must be deleted/mutated/eliminated; retinoblastoma two-hit, Lynch, Li-Fraumeni; NOT true of oncogenes), epistasis (allele of one gene affects expression of another; albinism, alopecia), aneuploidy (abnormal chromosome number; nondisjunction mitosis/meiosis; Down, Turner 45,XO, oncogenesis).
- **p77 terms**: dominant negative mutation (heterozygote makes nonfunctional altered protein that PREVENTS normal product from functioning — mutated p53 binds DNA and blocks wild-type p53 from promoter), linkage disequilibrium (alleles co-occur more/less often than chance; measured in POPULATION not family; varies between populations; HLA, CFTR), mosaicism (genetically distinct cell lines; somatic = mitotic error post-fertilization propagates through tissues; germline/gonadal = egg/sperm only; suspect gonadal mosaicism when parents/relatives unaffected; McCune-Albright = GNAS activating, unilateral café-au-lait with ragged edges + polyostotic fibrous dysplasia + endocrinopathy; lethal if pre-fertilization, survivable as mosaic), locus heterogeneity (different loci → same disease; albinism, retinitis pigmentosa, familial hypercholesterolemia), allelic heterogeneity (different mutations same locus → same disease; β-thalassemia), heteroplasmy (normal + mutated mtDNA → variable expression; Leber hereditary optic neuropathy; mtDNA from mother to ALL children), uniparental disomy (2 copies of a chromosome from 1 parent; heterodIsomy = meiosis I error; isodIsomy = meiosis II error or postzygotic duplication+loss; UPD is euploid; most UPD → normal phenotype; consider isodisomy when recessive disorder manifests with only ONE carrier parent; examples PWS and AS).
- **population genetics p77**: bottleneck effect (fitness equal → natural disaster removes alleles by CHANCE → new frequency; founder effect = bottleneck from calamitous population separation), natural selection (fitness-increasing alleles passed down), genetic drift (dramatic chance shift in allelic frequency; founder + bottleneck are both drift).
- **HWE p78**: random mating → allele and genotype frequencies constant; p + q = 1; p² = homozygosity A; q² = homozygosity a; 2pq = heterozygosity (carrier frequency if AR); p² + 2pq + q² = 1; X-linked recessive: males = q, females = q². Assumptions: no mutation, no natural selection, completely random mating, no net migration, large population. Rare AR diseases: p ≈ 1. Worked example: CF prevalence 1/3200 → q² = 1/3200 → q ≈ 0.017 (1.7%); p = 1 − √1/3200 ≈ 0.982; 2pq ≈ 0.035 (3.5% carriers).
- **imprinting p78**: one gene copy silenced by methylation, only the other expressed → parent-of-origin effects; expressed copy may be mutated, not expressed, or deleted. PWS: MATERNALLY derived genes silenced (except UBE3A); disease when PATERNAL allele deleted/mutated; hyperphagia, obesity, intellectual disability, hypogonadism, hypotonia; chromosome 15 paternal origin; 25% of cases = maternal UPD. AS: PATERNALLY derived UBE3A silenced; disease when MATERNAL allele deleted/mutated; Hand-flapping, Ataxia, severe Intellectual disability, inappropriate Laughter, Seizures (HAILS the Angels); UBE3A on maternal chromosome 15; 5% = paternal UPD. Mnemonics: POP = Prader-Willi, Obesity/overeating, Paternal allele deleted; MAMAS = Maternal allele deleted, Angelman, Mood, Ataxia, Seizures.
