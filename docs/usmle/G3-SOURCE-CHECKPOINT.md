## Key facts per topic (from text layer)
- **p83 trisomies**: screened 1st/2nd trimester with noninvasive prenatal tests; incidence Down (21) > Edwards (18) > Patau (13); autosomal monosomies incompatible with life (high chance of recessive trait expression). Down: ID, flat facies, prominent epicanthal folds, single palmar crease, incurved 5th finger, gap between 1st 2 toes, duodenal atresia, Hirschsprung, CHD (eg, AVSD), Brushfield spots (whitish spots at periphery of iris); early-onset Alzheimer (chromosome 21 codes for amyloid precursor protein), ↑risk AML/ALL. 95% meiotic nondisjunction (most commonly meiosis I; ↑ with advanced maternal age 1:1500 <20 → 1:25 >45); 4% unbalanced Robertsonian (typically 14;21); 1% postfertilization mitotic error. "Drinking age (21)"; most common viable chromosomal disorder + most common cause of genetic intellectual disability. 1st-trimester US: ↑nuchal translucency, hypoplastic nasal bone; markers "hi up": ↑hCG, ↑inhibin. ↑risk umbilical hernia. The 6 As: Atlantoaxial instability, Advanced maternal age, Atresia (duodenal), Atrioventricular septal defect, Alzheimer disease (early onset), AML (<5y)/ALL (>5y). Edwards: PRINCE Edward — Prominent occiput, Rocker-bottom feet, Intellectual disability, Nondisjunction, Clenched fists with overlapping fingers, low-set Ears, micrognathia, CHD (eg, VSD), omphalocele, myelomeningocele; death usually by age 1. "Election age (18)"; 2nd most common autosomal trisomy in live birth; every prenatal screening marker decreases. Patau: severe ID, rocker-bottom feet, microphthalmia, microcephaly, cleft lip/palate, holoprosencephaly, polydactyly, cutis aplasia, congenital heart (pump) disease, polycystic kidney disease, omphalocele; death usually by age 1. "Puberty at age 13"; defect in fusion of prechordal mesoderm → midline defects.
- **p84 chromosome map**: 3 von Hippel-Lindau, renal cell carcinoma; 4 ADPKD (PKD2), achondroplasia, Huntington; 5 Cri-du-chat, FAP; 6 hemochromatosis (HFE); 7 Williams, CF; 9 Friedreich ataxia, tuberous sclerosis (TSC1); 11 Wilms tumor, β-globin defects (sickle, β-thal), MEN1, ataxia telangiectasia, Ewing sarcoma t(11;22); 13 Patau, Wilson, retinoblastoma (RB1), BRCA2; 15 Prader-Willi, Angelman, Marfan; 16 ADPKD (PKD1), α-globin defects (α-thal), TSC2; 17 NF1, BRCA1, TP53 (Li-Fraumeni); 18 Edwards; 21 Down; 22 NF2, DiGeorge (22q11); X fragile X, Turner (XO), XLA, Klinefelter (XXY).
- **p84 Robertsonian**: commonly involves chromosome pairs 21, 22, 13, 14, 15; one of the most common types of translocation; long arms of 2 acrocentric chromosomes fuse at the centromere, 2 short arms lost. Balanced (no significant gain/loss) → normally no abnormal phenotype. Unbalanced (missing/extra genes) → miscarriage, stillbirth, chromosomal imbalance (eg, Down, Patau).
- **p84 cri-du-chat**: cry of the cat; congenital deletion on short arm of chromosome 5 (46,XX or XY, 5p−); microcephaly, moderate-severe ID, high-pitched crying, epicanthal folds, cardiac abnormalities (VSD); "I cry when I am Very SaD."
- **p85 Williams**: congenital microdeletion of long arm of chromosome 7 (deleted region includes elastin gene); "elfin" facies, ID, hypercalcemia, well-developed verbal skills, extreme friendliness with strangers, cardiovascular problems (supravalvular aortic stenosis, pulmonary artery stenosis, renal artery stenosis).
